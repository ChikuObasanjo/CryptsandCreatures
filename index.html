            Ixchel's plaza board

            Patolli

            Six pieces, six matched wagers. The first to claim four wins the match.

            0 gp

            Ixchel plays Patolli by the plaza's own rules, with gold riding on every piece.

            Toss five beans. One to four marked beans move that many spaces; five marked beans move ten. A piece leaves reserve only on a toss of exactly one, landing on its own start square. A toss of zero passes the turn.

            Squares are numbered from your own START, so your pieces always count upward from 1 to 52 and then go HOME; the faint chevrons show the way. You must land exactly at HOME; a move that overshoots is illegal. No piece may share a space. You may capture one opposing piece only on a gold center crossroads; it returns to reserve and its matched wager is claimed.

            Every arm has two penalty triangles, one in each lane. Landing on one sends one wager unit to the pot; if that piece's wager is already spent, the piece leaves the board and the other player claims its wager. Landing on any of the eight rounded end squares grants another turn. Here, six matched gold wagers are played as one short match: the first to claim four wins; if all six resolve without a majority, it is a draw. This wager-and-scoring system is Ixchel's local variation. Gold is fictional, and no expedition time passes.
