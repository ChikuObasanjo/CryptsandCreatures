Crypts & Creatures: Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Crypts & Creatures is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. This policy covers the Android app and the web version.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your saved game, settings (language, typeface, dark mode, sound, controls), and progress are stored locally on your device so you can continue later.
  • This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage or the browser's site data, deletes it.

Internet access

The Android app carries the standard internet permission of its web engine, but the game is fully playable offline and does not send your data anywhere.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address listed on the game's Google Play page.
